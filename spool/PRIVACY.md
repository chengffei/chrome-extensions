# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-01

Spool collects no personal data. There is no Spool server.

## What Spool accesses

- **On bilibili video pages, when you click the toolbar icon:** Spool asks bilibili's own API for the video's details and stream list. The request is made from the page, so your browser attaches your bilibili cookies to it the same way the bilibili player does. Spool never reads, stores or sends those cookies anywhere else.
- **The video and audio streams:** downloaded from bilibili's CDN and written to the file you choose on your computer.
- **Your codec choice:** stored with `chrome.storage.local` on this device only.

## What Spool does not do

- No analytics, telemetry, crash reports or advertising
- No requests to any server other than bilibili's
- No access to other websites, your browsing history or other tabs
- No remote code: everything runs from the files in the extension package

## Contact

Open an issue at <https://github.com/chengffei/chrome-extensions/issues>.

# 隐私政策

更新日期：2026-11-01

Spool 不收集任何个人数据，也没有 Spool 服务器。

## 访问了什么

- **在 B 站视频页、你点击工具栏图标时：** Spool 向 B 站自己的接口请求视频信息和清晰度列表。请求从页面发出，浏览器会像 B 站播放器一样自动带上你的 B 站 Cookie。Spool 不读取、不保存、也不把 Cookie 发往别处。
- **音视频流：** 从 B 站 CDN 下载，写入你在电脑上选择的文件。
- **编码选择：** 用 `chrome.storage.local` 只存在本机。

## 不做什么

- 无统计、遥测、崩溃上报和广告
- 除 B 站外不请求任何服务器
- 不访问其他网站、浏览历史或其他标签页
- 无远程代码：所有代码都在插件包内

## 联系

在 <https://github.com/chengffei/chrome-extensions/issues> 提 issue。
